Darcy's Law — linear flow form
Q = k · A · ΔP / (μ · L)
Q = flow rate (cc/s) k = permeability (darcy) A = cross-section area (cm²) ΔP = pressure drop (atm) μ = viscosity (cp) L = length (cm)

What Darcy's Law tells us
Q ∝ k: double perm → double flow rate
Q ∝ ΔP: double drawdown → double rate
Q ∝ 1/μ: heavy oil flows 1,000× slower
Q ∝ 1/L: longer reservoir → lower rate
Q ∝ A: bigger wellbore contact → more flow
Fracking creates A. Horizontal wells create L⁻¹.
